## ⚡ About Me

UK-based Technical Product Manager. Started in bid writing, then decided building the products was more fun than writing about them.

I design and ship AI-powered agentic workflows, open-source utilities, and micro-SaaS that solve real business problems — living at the intersection of engineering, business, and user experience, turning complicated ideas into structured solutions.

- 🔭 Building **Open Tender Agents** — autonomous bidding for UK public tenders
- 🌱 Exploring AWS, backend APIs, and multi-agent orchestration
- 💬 Ask me about **Python, Docker, and Docs as Code**
- 📍 London, UK
